Raw Clinical Dataset Overview

Initial tabular preview of patient records displaying demographic features, admission and discharge timestamps, diagnosis descriptions, clinical specialty categorization, medication counts, and readmission indicators before preprocessing.

Dataset Metadata & Feature Definitions
  • HICSS_ID: Unique anonymous patient encounter identifier
  • Gender / Age: Patient demographic information (M/F) and age at admission
  • Ethnic Group Description: Self-reported patient ethnicity
  • POSTCODE.1: Outward postal area code for geographic clustering
  • Readmission: Target binary indicator (True = Readmitted within 30 days)
  • Admission / Discharge Date: Timestamps recorded for the hospital episode
  • Discharge_day / Alert Status: Day of week and alert severity color coding
  • Admitting Ward / Admission Method: Clinical department and entry route (e.g., Emergency GP, A&E)
  • Admission Source / Discharge Method: Pre-admission origin and clinical disposition
  • Discharge Destination Desc: Post-discharge residence (e.g., Usual Place of Residence)
  • Type of Patient / Length of Stay (Days): Inpatient status type and total duration of stay
  • Primary Diagnosis Code / Description: Primary ICD-10 clinical diagnostic coding
  • Main Specialty Description: Attending clinical medical specialty
  • Medications: Total number of distinct active medications prescribed
  • Lives alon / SCC / Follow-up: Social indicators (Lives alone, Social Care, Follow-up scheduled)
  • Comorbidities: Quantified sum of co-existing medical conditions
1. Target Class Imbalance

The dataset contains 2,549 Non-Readmitted cases (94.1%) and 159 Readmitted cases (5.9%). This extreme 16:1 class imbalance required oversampling via SMOTE during model training to avoid positive class bias.

2. Correlation Matrix Heatmap

Highlights pairwise Pearson correlations. Key predictors like Length_of_Stay_Encoded (0.58), Patient Type Encoded (0.90 with Admission Method), and Comorbidities show strong internal colinearity, driving overall model weight distribution.

3. Main Diagnosis Groups (Post-Mapping)

Mapped ICD-10 groupings show primary patient volume concentrated in Categories I (514 cases), K (433 cases), and J (280 cases), corresponding to Circulatory, Digestive, and Respiratory diseases respectively.

4. Diagnosis Groups by Gender

Compares patient volume across primary ICD groups by gender. Male patients (M) show higher prevalence in Group I (313 vs 201) and Group K (221 vs 212), whereas females (F) exceed males in Group S and Group H.

5. Specialty Distribution by Gender

General Medicine leads all clinical departments (287 Males, 297 Females), followed by Cardiology (179 M, 118 F) and Geriatric Medicine (132 M, 173 F).

6. Missing Value Analysis

Identified missingness in Medications (1,184), SCC (653), Follow-up (653), and Lives alon (526), requiring imputation prior to model training.

7. Outlier Analysis (IQR Method)

Calculated IQR upper/lower bounds. Identified 301 outliers in Length of Stay (>17.5 days) and 239 in Medications (>12.5), which were scaled via StandardScaler.

8. Discharge Destination Encoding

Verified categorical encoding where USUAL PLACE OF RESIDENCE maps to 0, and non-standard discharge destinations map to 1.

9. Association Rule Mining (Apriori)

Displays the strongest clinical association rules linked to patient readmission. Combinations involving Admitting Ward Category Encoded_0 alongside Follow-up_1 or Lives alon_1 achieve the highest Lift metrics (~2.21 - 2.22) with 13.0% - 13.1% confidence, identifying high-risk clinical profiles.

10. Model Evaluation & ROC-AUC Curves

Compares SMOTE oversampling against Class-Weighted baselines for Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost. SMOTE-trained Logistic Regression leads performance with an AUC of 0.7766, followed by Random Forest (AUC = 0.7447) and XGBoost (AUC = 0.7157).
