Welcome to the Hospital Readmission Predictor

This tool uses machine learning to predict whether a patient will be readmitted within 30 days of discharge.

Enter patient data on the Predict page to get an instant prediction. Learn more about the project on the About page.

Start Prediction

Why Predict Readmission?

Reducing hospital readmissions improves patient outcomes and lowers healthcare costs. Early identification of at-risk patients allows for better discharge planning and follow-up care.

Data-Driven

Our model is trained on historical patient data, including demographics, lab results, and procedure counts, to provide accurate predictions.

How It Works

Fill in the form on the Predict page, and our algorithm will output a risk score and probability of readmission.
